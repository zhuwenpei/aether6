7. Data provenance statement
----------------------------
The 3.7.8 database is an offline, reanalysis/climatology-inspired typical-process model calibrated to public ERA5, NCEP/NCAR, NOAA OISST/TCHP and WMO/NOAA tropical-cyclone guidance. It is not a bundled raw ERA5/OISST analysis for a particular date and is not a live weather feed. This limitation is explicitly documented in RESEARCH_BASIS_3.7.8.md and UPDATE_NOTES_3.7.8.md.

Final archive integrity is checked separately after ZIP creation with unzip -t and SHA-256.
