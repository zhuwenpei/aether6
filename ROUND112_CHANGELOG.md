# ROUND112 CHANGELOG — AETHER 6.0.35

## Synoptic wind-field cleanup

This round addresses the residual marine artifacts still visible in the user screenshots generated by the updated synoptic product code.

### Marine seam / wedge suppression

- Added a new ocean-only cleanup pass in `SynopticWindField.ts` that runs after the boundary-layer and terrain-wake repair stages.
- The repair compares the resolved 10 m wind against the pre-solver reference field and the surrounding marine neighborhood, then suppresses obviously spurious narrow wedges, low-wind seams, and banded coastal stripes without flattening the cyclone core.
- The correction is strongest in the outer TC circulation and fragmented coastal waters, and it is bounded so it does not alter the storm center annotation or eyewall intensity calibration.

### Wind-map label cleanup

- The synoptic wind-field product now keeps only the primary tropical-cyclone centre `L` marker.
- Extra synoptic `H`/`L` markers and non-primary cyclone-center `L` markers are no longer calculated or drawn.

### Quality

- No reduction was made to wind-grid resolution, contour fidelity, raster resolution, or interpolation quality.
